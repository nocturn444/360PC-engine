# examples/rooms

A run of ten floors generated by `engine/modules/procgen` (`procgen.floors`, seed 1) walked room
by room with `engine/modules/rooms`, by the soft-body slime of [examples/slime](../slime/README.md).

- Floor 1 has 11 or 12 rooms and floor 10 has 21 or 22. Each floor has a key that opens its lock,
  a treasure room, a shop (except on the last floor), sometimes a miniboss (from floor 2,
  likelier the deeper the run goes), and a boss far from the start. The special types are
  `procgen.roomtypes.defaults()` plus the wave 2 types in `rooms_spawn.lua` (`rooms_data.types()`),
  and their rooms are one-door templates in `rooms_data.lua`.
- The run also spawns the new special rooms from `rooms_spawn.lua`: a casino (replaces the shop),
  a super treasure (replaces the treasure after one was skipped), a pact, one or two trap arenas
  (they seal like a miniboss until their enemies are dead) and an obstacle course. Their chances
  change from floor to floor with what happened: a shop left unentered makes the casino likelier,
  damage makes the obstacle likelier, a trap resets its chance, and a missed treasure opens the
  super treasure. Their contents (stock, pedestals, casino, larvae, rewards) are not wired yet:
  for now they are only layouts.
- The floors go through biomes (`rooms_biomes.lua`, `procgen.biomes`), always in the same
  order: forest (floors 1-2), deep jungle (3-4), grotto (5), fungal cave (6-7), catacombs (8),
  labs (9) and catastrophe (10). Each biome has a difficulty, its own enemies (health, bite, colour), wall
  colours, a music track name and a few rooms more or fewer. The HUD shows its name, and the bottom left corner shows the FPS and the build version (`rooms_version.lua`). Each biome's
  mob has an id (`rooms_biomes.lua`), so the content pools can vary by mob.
- A boss or miniboss room shuts its doors while its enemies are alive: the slime has to win the
  fight (or fall) before it can leave. The first clear gives one mutation, and the boss opens a
  stairway. Going down it, behind a fade, leads to the next floor, a new map. The slime keeps its
  health and everything the run gave it. Clearing the boss of the last floor (10) wins the run:
  no stairs appear there, and after a fade a placeholder ending cutscene plays
  (`rooms_endings.lua`, `rooms_cutscene.lua`) before the end-of-run menu opens with the title
  "Victory" (`docs/CUTSCENES.md`, `docs/MENUS.md`).
- The minimap colours the room types (boss red, treasure gold, shop green, miniboss orange, the
  wave 2 types their own colours and icons) through `rooms_minimap.lua`. The floor number and the
  run's resources are shown top left.

## Art

The "8-bit hi-fi" look: simple pixel art made rich by light, parallax and particles, drawn at the
320x180 internal resolution with 16 px tiles.

```
art/
  palette.png, palette.hex         the 32-colour master palette
  <biome>/sky.png                  fixed sky, 320x180
  <biome>/bg_far.png               far parallax layer, scrolls at 0.25 of the camera
  <biome>/bg_mid.png               mid parallax layer, scrolls at 0.5 of the camera
  <biome>/tiles.png                4x4 autotile sheet, 16x16 per variant
  fx/glow.png                      additive light for the fireflies
  enemies/mob_walk.png             4 walk frames of 16x16
```

`rooms_art.lua` loads a biome's images once (cached, nearest filtering) and builds one
SpriteBatch per room from the solid tiles; the variant is picked from the open sides (mask 1
up, 2 right, 4 down, 8 left; outside the room counts as solid). One-way ledges stay the flat
rectangles. A biome without art keeps the older flat wall drawing. Every room gets fireflies in
its biome's `glow` colour (`rooms_biomes.lua`), and enemies use `mob_walk.png` (bosses and
minibosses at 2x).

`tools/art/gen_placeholders.py` (PC only, needs Pillow) regenerates the placeholder PNGs. Each
file is a stand-in: hand-drawn art replaces it by keeping the same file name and size, and the
file can then be listed in the script's `KEEP` set so a rerun leaves it alone.

## Stats and mutations

Pending the owner's confirmation: the stats, the virus currency, the shield, the machines and the
cosmetics below are example designs chosen by an AI agent, not decisions yet (AGENTS.md, section 9).

The six run stats are `luck`, `jump`, `speed`, `damage`, `shield` and `health`
(`rooms_stats.lua`, [docs/STATS.md](docs/STATS.md)). Bases: luck 5 (0..10), jump and speed
multipliers 1.0, damage 1, shield capacity 0, health 5. Jump and speed scale movement; damage
scales the three attacks (`power` in `rooms_attacks.lua`); health is the maximum, not the current
health.

The treasure room holds exactly one mutation, drawn for the floor and the current build from the
content pools (`rooms_content.lua`, `rooms_mutations.lua`,
[docs/CONTENT.md](docs/CONTENT.md)). It is not a choice: touching it is enough. Mutations
are unique or stackable, can require a build tag, can gain weight from synergy tags, and can be
restricted by biome, floor, room or mob. The three example mutations are a jump/antenna change, a
shield/shell change and a speed/legs change; they are placeholders to be replaced.

Every mutation also changes the slime's look (`rooms_look.lua`,
[docs/COSMETICS.md](docs/COSMETICS.md)): colour, eyes, shape or new limbs. A mutation with
`body` changes the soft body's rest shape, so its hitbox follows; one without it only changes the
drawing. The legs example is wider and faster; it does not add a new walking model.

The shield is a resource, not flat armour: damage hits the shield first, overflow goes to health,
and after `5 + 2.5 * capacity` seconds without a hit it refills. The HUD shows `SH current/max`.

## Consumables, virus and machines

- A normal heart heals 1; at full health it becomes one virus. Hearts can drop from mobs and are
  in the start room for testing.
- Virus is the run's currency. It comes from broken crates, a chance when a room is cleared, and
  (as a hook) future machines; it is spent at machines that accept money. The start room has a
  small virus pickup.
- Breakable crates are props (`rooms_props.lua`, `engine.modules.props`): attack them in front or
  fall on them. A broken crate stays broken while the room exists and can drop virus (its own rng
  stream). The crate's type and fields (`health`, `virus_chance`, `virus`) are in that file.
- The shop template has a test healer (`rooms_machines.lua`): `accepts_money = true`, 5 virus,
  one use, fills the health. Each machine has its own state. Interact with it when close.
- Pools, drops and pickups are described in [docs/ECONOMY.md](docs/ECONOMY.md) and
  [docs/CONTENT.md](docs/CONTENT.md). The shop stock is deliberately not implemented yet.

## Rooms and movement

- Only the current room exists: its collision grid is built from the template's rows when the
  slime comes in, with the border opened where the map put a door.
- The camera stays inside the room (`camera:setBounds(nav:bounds())`) and follows the slime;
  rooms of two cells scroll.
- Maps branch in both directions: `rooms_data.lua` has rooms with up and down exits (a cross,
  a shaft, T junctions and corners), and the border opens the middle three columns of the top
  or bottom row for those doors. Together with `procgen`'s `branching` (0.5 by default) the
  floor spreads into branches instead of a single long corridor.
- Crossing a door starts a 0.3 s fade (`rooms.transition`); the room is swapped while the screen
  is black, and the slime arrives inside the next room by the door it came through.
- The door into the second zone is locked (gold) until the slime touches the key in the vault
  room; the room is then rebuilt with the door open.
- Enemies (`rooms_enemies.lua`): platformer controller bodies in a bump world made from the
  room's grid, each run by a patrol / chase / attack state machine (`combat.behaviours`), in one
  combat world with the slime, which combat sees through `softbody.box`. Landing on an enemy
  while falling fast hurts it and bounces the slime off; a bite hurts the slime and knocks it
  back, and it blinks while it cannot be hurt. At 0 HP the run ends: after a fade the game over
  menu opens over the frozen room (Restart Run, Main Menu, Stats). A room remembers its enemies:
  the dead stay dead and a hurt enemy keeps its health while the run lasts.
- An enemy that falls out of the room through a door moves to the room on the other side and
  turns up there the next time it is loaded, keeping its health. Past an edge with no door, or an
  exit that leads nowhere, it dies. Neither counts as a kill or drops anything.
- Props (`rooms_props.lua`, `engine.modules.props`): the hall has spikes, a statue and a wide
  platform; some crates sit around the templates. Props with `solid` or `one_way` collision are
  stamped on the room grid, so the slime, enemies and attacks collide with them. Spikes cost a
  heart and knock the slime away; a statue fires its attack on the enemy team every interval. The
  slime drapes over the edge of a hangable platform (`=`, the `platform` prop), but slides off the
  edges of a wide platform, which is not hangable.
- The slime's attacks (`rooms_attacks.lua`, example `combat.attacks` configs): a melee arc in
  front of it, a ranged shot, and a bomb thrown low that bounces, blows on touching an enemy
  or after its fuse, and pushes everything in its radius away. One mode at a time, switched
  with the switch button; the mode is kept from room to room.
- Vines (`rooms_vines.lua`): a `vine` prop is grabbed by pressing up or down when the slime's
  centre is on it; up/down climb at the prop's `speed` with gravity cancelled; left/right pump
  a damped pendulum (when `swing` is on) limited to a maximum angle; jump lets go with the
  pendulum's tangential speed plus the jump, and down at the bottom end also lets go. On the
  keyboard up shares jump's keys, so holding up climbs without letting go. The tall room has one.
- A minimap in the corner shows the explored rooms (`nav:minimap()`), the current one in yellow.

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left / Right, A / D | Left stick, d-pad |
| Jump | Space, Up, W | A |
| Grab / climb a vine | Up / Down, W / S | D-pad up / down, left stick up / down |
| Squash (let go on the ground: a higher jump) | Down, S | d-pad down |
| Attack | X, J | X |
| Switch attack (melee, ranged, explosive) | C, K, Tab | Y |
| Interact (machine) | E, F | B |
| Pause (Resume, Options, Restart run, Main Menu) | Escape, P | Start |
| Quit | -- | Hold Back 3 s (console) |

## Menus

The game starts on the title menu (`rooms_title.lua`): Start Run, Challenges, Progress, Options
and Exit, with each sub page opening on its Back button and Exit asking first (No is focused).
Start Run enters the play state. Progress lists the completion percentage and the Bestiary,
Biomes, Achievements, Unlocked items, Endings and Runs pages; Options has the music and SFX
sliders and, on PC only, a fullscreen toggle. The Endings page shows `???` until an ending is
seen, then its name; selecting a seen one replays its cutscene. Debug builds (or a `-test` build
version) list every ending as playable under "Endings (PREVIEW)". When the slime dies,
`rooms_play` pushes the game over menu (`rooms_gameover.lua`) over the frozen room: Restart Run
starts again at floor 1, Main Menu clears the stack and returns to the title, and Stats shows
the run's floor, kills, time in m:ss (`play.run_time`), virus, the current streak and what the
run discovered for the first time. Clearing the last floor's boss plays the picked ending
cutscene, records it in the profile (also when skipped with a 1 s hold of Back) and then opens
the same menu titled "Victory" with the play state still under it. The menu and cutscene states
set `autopause = false`, so losing the controller never pushes the pause menu over them. The
menus are `engine.ui` screens drawn with `engine.text.font(1)` and fit the 320 x 180 screen.

`rooms_pause.lua` is a transparent state over the play state: while it is open the play state does
not update. It has Resume, Options, Restart run and Main Menu; both destructive actions ask first
(No is focused), and Main Menu clears the stack without recording a loss. If the controller in
use is disconnected (or the PC window loses the focus), `engine.autopause` opens it with a
message; reconnecting the controller updates the message, and A resumes.

The permanent profile (`rooms_profile.lua`, `profile.sav` through `engine.save`) stores the
discovered enemies, bosses and biomes, the kills and deaths by type, the run record and streak,
the seen endings, the empty achievement/unlocked sets and the options. It is loaded once at
startup and written only on a first discovery, a first ending, at the end of a run and when
leaving the Options page. `docs/MENUS.md` and `docs/CUTSCENES.md` have the details.

The rooms are in `rooms_data.lua` as rows of strings, the shape LDtk levels read by
`engine.level` also give. Run it on PC from the repository root: `love examples/rooms`.

The HUD is provisional: HP, virus, shield/luck, floor/biome and the last mutation are text drawn
with `engine.text`, so it works on the console without a TTF; the pause menu uses
`engine.text.font(1)` as its theme font for the same reason.

What to look at on the console: the fade hides the room change (no frame of the old or new
room in the wrong place), the camera never shows outside the room, the extra limbs stay inside
the pixel grid, and the frame stays at 30 FPS in the two-by-two room with its three enemies.
